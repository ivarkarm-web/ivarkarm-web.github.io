# Pionier audio architecture

Pionier uses procedural synthesis for every playable instrument voice. The handpan voice is generated by the local sound library; scale and root selection change the generated pitches without loading recorded one-shots.

## Shipped audio

- **Instrument voices:** synthesized locally through the Web Audio API
- **Bedroom Sessions:** the owner's recorded soundscapes, streamed from the archive.org collection; these are the only musical recordings used by the app
- **Looper:** captures the app's live output in the browser and exports user-created loops

No handpan note samples, sample manifests, chopped note packs, or third-party instrument recordings are bundled or requested at runtime.

## Audio changes

Do not add a note-sample bank or ship handpan one-shots without an explicit product decision. Keep synthesized voices available offline and test the first note after audio unlock on iOS Safari and Android Chrome.
