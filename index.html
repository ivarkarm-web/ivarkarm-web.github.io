About

Street performer, improviser, and sonic wanderer.

02 — The Story

The Story

2008 – 2012 · Berlin

Teenage years, squatting abandoned buildings, busking metro stations. Rosenthaler Platz, Alexanderplatz, Warschauer Brücke.

2013 – 2020 · Travels

Traveled through Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, Poland, Latvia, Lithuania, Estonia. Hitchhiking, buses, tents, urban parks.

Currently · Greece

Living in Greece. Streets are my stage, handpan in hand.

03 — Manifesto

Manifesto

I don't separate life from music.

What I play comes from how I live—moving, searching, messing up, starting again.

The street is my stage because it's real. No tickets, no barriers.

If you stop, you're part of it. If you listen, we share something.

I don't chase perfection. I chase moments—where sound, place, and people line up.
