About

Street performer, improviser, and sonic wanderer.

From the streets of Berlin to the corners of Athens, creating moments where sound and life converge.

The Story

2008 — Berlin & Finding a Voice

Teenage years in Berlin, squatting in abandoned buildings, busking in metro stations, and drumming on streets that never slept. Rosenthaler Platz, Alexanderplatz, Warschauer Brücke—cycling between spots, chasing crowds, dodging cops.

2015 — Travels Across Europe

Curiosity and the streets carried me through Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, and back to Estonia. Tents, trains, urban parks, and random city corners became stages. The journey itself became the destination.

2023 — Athens & Today

Living and performing in Athens for three years now. Streets are still my stage, handpan and drums in hand, improvisation guiding every note. The search continues.

Manifesto

I don't separate life from music.

What I play comes from how I live—moving, searching, messing up, starting again.

The street is my stage because it's real. No tickets, no barriers, no pretending.

If you stop, you're part of it. If you listen, we share something.

I don't chase perfection. I chase moments—those seconds where sound, place, and people line up and something honest happens.

Music, to me, is a language. You don't need to know it, just feel it.
